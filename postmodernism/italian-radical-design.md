# Italian Radical Design / Anti-design

[Home](../README.md)

## What is it?

Italian Radical Design emerged in the 1960s through groups including Archizoom and Superstudio. It questioned assumptions about consumption, urban planning, and the designer's social role. It belongs here as an important precursor and overlapping strand of postmodern design, rather than a claim that every radical project called itself postmodern. Some projects were deliberately speculative instead of intended for construction. [Historical context](https://www.mfah.org/press/mfah-revisits-revolutionary-italian-design-collection-dennis-freedman).

## How do you recognize or use it?

- An object or image can question a design convention rather than solve a conventional brief.
- Exaggeration and unexpected material combinations can function as criticism.
- Drawings, photomontages, and speculative environments are important media.
- Apparently rational systems may be pushed to an extreme to reveal their consequences.

**Suggested application:** For a hypothetical exhibition about housing, a deliberately exaggerated collage could raise a question about uniform development. Label speculation clearly and provide reliable information alongside it. Radical imagery is useful for provoking discussion; it is not a substitute for workable navigation.

### Example 1: The Continuous Monument: On the River, project (Perspective)

- **Creator:** Superstudio; collaborators listed individually in MoMA's record.
- **Title:** *The Continuous Monument: On the River, project (Perspective)*.
- **Date:** 1969.
- **Institution:** MoMA, New York.
- **Direct source and image:** [View the work and institutional record](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/934?artist_id=5733&page=1&sov_referrer=artist).

**What it demonstrates:** This conceptual project extends an immense geometric structure through a landscape. Its scale turns orderly architecture into a question about totalizing systems. It is an unbuilt proposal represented through an image, not a photograph of a completed monument.

**Image credit and reuse:** Collection and image source: MoMA. Check the record's rights and Licensing information before reproduction.

### Example 2: Mies chair and footstool

- **Creator:** Archizoom Associati; manufactured by Poltronova.
- **Title:** *Mies chair and footstool*.
- **Date:** Designed and made 1969.
- **Institution:** Powerhouse, Sydney.
- **Direct source and image:** [View the work and institutional record](https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/object/374595).

**What it demonstrates:** A sharply geometric frame meets an unconventional combination of rubber and other materials. The name invites comparison with Mies van der Rohe's modernist furniture. Read it as a critical engagement with established design language rather than simply another minimalist chair.

**Image credit and reuse:** Collection and image source: Powerhouse, object record 374595. Image reuse terms must be checked on the record; the image is linked rather than embedded.
